Panel fit

One whole panel,
never cut off

It reads the gaps between drawings to find where each panel starts and ends, then stops with the panel in the middle of your screen. A panel taller than the screen is shown in two steps, top then bottom.

Reading time

More text, longer stop.
Art only, shorter.

It counts the characters in the speech bubbles to decide how long to stay on a panel. You can change the timing whenever you like to match your reading speed.

Frequently asked questions

Which sites does it work on?

It turns on automatically on episode pages of WEBTOON (webtoons.com). Naver Webtoon and Kakao Webtoon, the Korean sites, are supported too. AutoWebtoon is not an official product of any of these companies, and it does not unlock paid episodes.

Does it work in other browsers like Brave?

On a PC, please use Chrome. In Brave, full screen doesn't turn on and panels don't line up. Other browsers haven't been checked yet.

How do I pause or change the speed?

Tap the comic once to pause; a control bar appears. Adjust the art time and the text time, then tap again to continue. On a PC the space bar works too.

Does it work in the WEBTOON app?

No. Apps don't let other programs read their screen. On a phone, open the webtoon site in Firefox instead.

Do you collect my data or what I read?

No. The only thing saved is your own timing settings, and they are not sent anywhere. See the privacy policy for details.

Does it work on iPhone?

Yes. Get AutoWebtoon on the App Store, turn it on in Settings → Apps → Safari → Extensions, then read webtoons in Safari.

What if I lose my key?

Contact us with your order ID. If you lost your key, enter your order ID under Get my key again and it is shown again right away.
